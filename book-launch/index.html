Press Release · A Morning of Word and Fellowship

Three books.
One morning.
A word unleashed.

SEALS OF LOVE premieres three new publications by Pastor Henry Oise, birthed to deepen the Church's prayer life and mobilize believers for global missions.

Read the release ↓ See the books

Reported by

SOL Media Team September 13, 2026 • 4 min read

To God be all the glory for the successful premiere of three new publications authored by Pastor Henry Oise.

The books, Sweet Hour of Prayer, SEND US OUT, and The Art of Praying The Scriptures, were unveiled on Saturday at a special gathering tagged "A Morning of Word and Fellowship," held in Abeokuta.

The event, which drew clergymen, missionaries, partners, and friends of the ministry from across the nation, featured a keynote address on the state of missions, insightful book reviews, and a dedication prayer led by the Vice-Chancellor of the Federal University of Agriculture, Abeokuta.

Who took part

  • Rev. Tunde AmosunKeynote Address
  • Prof. G.K. BabawaleBook Review
  • Pastor Peace ToluwadeBook Review
  • Pastor Edith OiseBook Review
  • Prof. Tunde KehindeDedication Prayer · VC, FUNAAB
  • Rt. Rev. J.O. BabajideChairman
  • Evang. E.A. Babajide (Mrs)Mother of the Day

Gratitude and purpose

In his remarks, Pastor Henry Oise expressed profound gratitude to all partners and guests for their unwavering support, and noted that the books were birthed to deepen the prayer life of the Church and mobilize believers for global missions.

"Birthed to deepen the prayer life of the Church, and mobilize believers for global missions."

SOL appreciates Realm of Glory International Church Choir for their ministration, Mrs. Eloviano Shodunke for moderating, and all volunteers and partners who contributed to the success of the programme.
