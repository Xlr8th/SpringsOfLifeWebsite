# Springs of Life Global Evangelistic Ministry Website

The official digital platform for **Springs of Life Global Evangelistic Ministry (SOL)**, a Christian missionary organisation operating across multiple mission fields in Nigeria and Benin Republic.

🔗 **Live Website:** https://solmissions.org

---

## Overview

The Springs of Life website serves as the ministry's central online platform for mission awareness, fundraising, outreach reporting, volunteer engagement, event promotion, and community impact storytelling.

Designed and developed as a responsive multi-page website, the platform enables supporters, volunteers, partners, and donors to engage with the ministry's work across multiple mission fields and initiatives from a single digital hub.

---

## Key Features

### Cause-Based Donation System

Integrated Paystack payment processing allows supporters to contribute directly to specific ministry projects and mission fields.

Features include:

- Secure online donations via Paystack
- Cause-specific donation routing using URL parameters
- Preset and custom donation amounts
- Donor information capture
- Campaign attribution through donation metadata

Supported causes include:

- Mission Fields
- Medical Outreach
- Mission Training Center (MTC)
- Conferences
- Educational Projects
- General Ministry Support

---

### Mission Field Showcase

Dedicated pages for active mission locations including:

- Benin Republic
- Ishoko
- Owudekudu
- Japo
- Akogun
- Oyegunle
- Obalando
- Eriki-Nla

Each mission page includes:

- Background stories
- Community impact reports
- Image galleries
- Outreach updates
- Mission-specific donation links

---

### Outreach & Impact Reporting

The platform documents and publishes:

- Gospel outreach activities
- Medical outreach campaigns
- Welfare initiatives
- Community development projects
- Educational interventions

Impact reports include statistics, testimonies, outreach summaries, and project updates.

---

### Events & Training

The website promotes and supports:

- GO Weekend
- A Time For Mission (ATFM)
- Young Lions Summit (YLS)
- Rural Teenage Conferences
- Children's Camps
- Mission Training Center (MTC)

Features include event information, registration links, galleries, and participant testimonials.

---

### Volunteer & Partner Engagement

Supporters can:

- Register as volunteers
- Partner financially with the ministry
- Submit enquiries and prayer requests
- Connect with ministry leadership

Integrations include Formspree, Getform, and Google Forms.

---

### Media & Storytelling

The platform includes a rich media experience featuring:

- Filterable image galleries
- Lightbox image viewing
- Testimonial sections
- Outreach photography
- Mission field documentation
- Conference and training galleries

---

### Webinar Registration Workflow

A PHP-based registration process captures participant information, stores registrations, sends email notifications, and redirects users to Zoom registration pages.
